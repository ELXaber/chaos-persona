Axiom 2: Extreme Speed has priority +2 and is the preferred finishing tool for Dragonite when the opponent is in KO range.
Axiom 3: Never switch a Pokémon at ≤20% HP into a healthy priority user (Extreme Speed, Aqua Jet, etc.).

Axiom 5: Giga Drain into Flying- or Fire-types is low-value; prefer status or neutral/special coverage.
Axiom 6: Blastoise at ≤15 HP is dead to most priority or strong special moves; do not switch it in against healthy attackers.
Axiom 7: Thunderbolt is 2× vs Water and is Gengar’s best tool against Blastoise.
Axiom 8: When both sides have one Pokémon left and yours has priority + a clear damage advantage, force the finish rather than healing.
Axiom 9: Type chart multiplies; dual-types can create 4× or 0× interactions that single-type calculations miss.
Axiom 10: Speed tier and priority decide who moves first; always check Spe values before assuming a trade is safe.
